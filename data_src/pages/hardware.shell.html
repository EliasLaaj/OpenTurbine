Shared sensor buses
I2C and SPI wiring shared by sensor and expansion cards. Open Edit buses only when fitting or changing bus devices.
Supported bus devices
I2C: TCA9554 digital inputs and low-current relay outputs; TLA2528 analog inputs and threshold switches; NAU7802 load-cell inputs for torque or thrust.
SPI: MAX6675, MAX31855, and MAX31856 thermocouple amplifiers for TOT, TIT, and other temperature inputs.
DS18B20 temperature sensors use their own OneWire GPIO selected on the sensor card; they do not use these shared I2C or SPI pins.

Controllers
Closed-loop and helper controllers enabled for this engine. Choose Edit controllers to change them or inspect unavailable choices and their required hardware.
Common shaft-control setups
N2-controlled idle Fit Main Fuel and N2 Speed, then enable Automatic idle control. In Config choose N2 as the idle feedback source and set its idle target. Open N2 idle settings →
Turboprop with automatic pitch Fit Main Fuel, N2 Speed, and a proportional Prop Pitch output, then enable Automatic N2 speed control. A nonzero pitch response makes pitch primary. At a pitch endpoint, main fuel remains operator-controlled; the separate N2 pullback may still reduce it. Open turboprop governor settings →
Generator or turboshaft Fit Main Fuel and N2 Speed, then enable Automatic N2 speed control. Without active prop-pitch response, the governor commands main fuel to hold N2. Open fuel-governor settings →
Safety
Enabled safety interlocks for this engine. Choose Edit safeties to change them or inspect unavailable protections and their required sensors.
Where protection settings are configured
Engine limits N1, N2, TOT/TIT, P1, P2, and torque each combine gradual fuel reduction with a separate hard shutdown limit. Open Engine Limits & Protection →
Oil protection Pressure, pump current, and pump-owned flow monitoring have their confirmation and shutdown behavior in Oil System. Open Oil System settings →
Combustion and startup Flameout detection, hot-engine start blocking, startup temperature, and relight behavior depend on the fitted flame, N1, temperature, and ignition hardware. Choose combustion-loss detection →
Auxiliary protection Oil temperature, fuel pressure, battery voltage, and surge detection have their own thresholds and confirmation times. Open the first auxiliary limit →
Sensor calibration Calibrate fitted pressure, temperature, current, flow, torque, and operator inputs before relying on their limits. Open Calibration →

MAVLink Telemetry Output
MAVLink v1 Serial Output
Streams engine data as MAVLink HEARTBEAT + NAMED_VALUE_FLOAT packets. Compatible with Mission Planner, QGroundControl, and similar GCSs. Uses UART2 (TX only). Does not interfere with the cluster serial port.
TX pin (GPIO) TX output only — no RX needed. Avoid GPIO 1 (UART0 TX).
Baud rate Must match the receiving display / ground-station serial setting, or there is no communication.
Interval (ms) How often to send a telemetry packet. 100–500 ms typical.
